Delete your Linezy account

Linezy, the receipt app by Linezy Digital OÜ. You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by e-mail.

In the app

  1. Open Linezy and tap the Settings (gear) icon on the home screen.
  2. Tap Privacy and data.
  3. Tap Delete account and data and type DELETE to confirm.

Deleting needs an internet connection. Your account and its data are deleted straight away, and the copy on your phone is wiped. On iPhone, an older copy can remain in your own iPhone backup (such as iCloud Backup), which is under your Apple account.

Without the app

E-mail privacy@linezy.app from your account's e-mail address (for Google sign-in, your Google address) and ask us to delete your account. If you can't, tell us and we'll find another way to confirm it's you. We delete the account and its data within one month and confirm it to you.

What is deleted

  • Your login.
  • Your receipts with their photos, items and warranties, including every receipt you added to a household you share.
  • Your scan records and your consents.
  • Your household, if you are its only member.
  • RevenueCat's records of your purchases.

What you delete is removed from our database and file storage straight away. On our current plan neither we nor our database provider keep backups.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A household that continues keeps the shop and product names its members taught the app. A warranty you added to another member's receipt stays with that receipt, without your name.
  • Withdrawals from a subscription: 3 years, so we can prove the withdrawal and refund in a dispute; if we paid the refund ourselves, the accounting details (date, amount, order number, reference) for 7 years, as the Estonian Accounting Act requires.
  • E-mails you sent us: about a complaint, a refund, a withdrawal or your rights, 3 years after our last reply; other e-mails, 1 year.
  • Service logs kept by our database provider, Supabase, for periods it sets.
  • Google Gemini keeps what it read for up to 55 days to detect abuse, then deletes it.
  • Shop and product names read from receipts stay in our catalog, no longer linked to you or your household.
  • Google Play and Apple keep their own records of your purchases.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium or Household subscription. Cancel it in the store where you bought it: on Android in the Play Store app (your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions), on iPhone in Settings, your name, then Subscriptions.

The full details are in section 8 of our privacy policy.
