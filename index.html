Every receipt, down to every line.

Scan a paper receipt or add a PDF. Linezy reads it into items and prices, so you can see what you spend and how prices change.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

From a crumpled receipt to your own price history

  1. Add a receipt

    Scan paper with your camera, or import a photo, screenshot or PDF. No signal? It waits on your phone.

  2. Linezy reads every line

    AI reads the shop, date, items, prices and discounts. It shows you what it isn't sure about, and you can correct anything.

  3. See where the money goes

    Spending by month, category and shop, and what each product has cost you over time.

What's in the app

  • Price history

    Every product you buy, per kg, liter or piece, with offers shown apart from regular prices.

  • Spending

    This month against the last, broken down by category and by shop.

  • Share with your household

    One shared list for up to 6 people. Any receipt can stay private to you.

  • Warranties

    Keep the warranty with its receipt and get a reminder on your phone before it runs out.

  • Export, always free

    Excel, CSV or PDF. Your receipts are yours to take with you.

  • Works without a connection

    Scans and corrections are saved on your phone first and sent when you're back online.

Receipts say a lot about you. They stay yours.

  • No ads, no selling

    No ads of any kind, and we never sell personal data.

  • No bank linking

    Linezy never asks for your bank. It only knows the receipts you add.

  • Stored in the EU

    Your receipts and account are stored in Frankfurt, Germany.

  • AI only if you say yes

    Receipts are read by Google Gemini, and only after you agree. Say no and you can still type receipts in by hand.

  • No analytics or tracking

    Not in the app and not on this website.

  • Delete whenever you want

    Delete a receipt or your whole account right in the app.
