1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Linezy Digital OÜ, registry code 17640652, VAT number EE103051875, Väike-Rae tee 11-5, Rae küla, Rae vald, 75310 Harju maakond, Estonia ("Linezy", "we", "us").

Questions about your data and requests about your rights: privacy@linezy.app. Anything else: support@linezy.app.

We have not appointed a data protection officer; we review this as Linezy grows. Messages to privacy@linezy.app reach the people at Linezy who are responsible for data protection.

2. In short

  • Linezy reads your shop receipts (photos and PDFs) into lists of items and prices, so you can see what you spend and how prices change. You can also type receipts in by hand.
  • Your receipts are stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Reading receipts with AI (Google Gemini) is optional and happens only with your explicit consent. Without it you can still type receipts in.
  • We never sell personal data. Linezy has no ads and no advertising or analytics tools, and does not link to bank accounts. Google receives: the photos and PDFs of receipts sent for AI reading with your consent; technical usage data from its document scanner on Android, only if you allow it; our e-mails with you, including the receipt we send when you withdraw from a subscription (Google Workspace); and, if you buy Premium on Android, the purchase (Google Play). See section 7.
  • Apart from the service providers who process data only for us (section 7), nobody sees your receipts except the members of the household you share them with (section 5 says what stays after you leave).
  • In the app you can download your receipts' details (Excel or CSV, or a PDF summary) and delete your account and its data at any time. For a copy of your photos, write to privacy@linezy.app.

3. What we store, why, and on what legal basis

"Contract" means the processing is needed to give you the service you asked for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). "Legitimate interest" means article 6(1)(f): we explain the interest each time, and you can object (section 9). "Explicit consent" means articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a).

  • Your account: your e-mail address, and the records of your sign-ins (time, IP address, and the app and phone type your phone reports) Why: To sign you in with the sign-in e-mail we send you, to keep your account safe, and to contact you about your account and these documents Legal basis: Contract; for sign-in records, our legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure
  • Receipts you add: photos and PDFs (one receipt can have several photos), and receipts you type in Why: Your receipt list, and so you can look at the original Legal basis: Contract. Because receipts can show health data, we also ask for your explicit consent to store them (section 4)
  • What was read or typed: shop name and address, city, date and time, items, quantities, prices, discounts, loyalty money used, totals, VAT, payment method (such as card or cash), categories, invoice due dates, how sure the reading was, and your corrections Why: Your receipts, spending, price history, savings, insights and export Legal basis: Contract. Because receipts can show health data, we also ask for your explicit consent to store them (section 4)
  • AI reading (optional): the photos or PDF of a receipt you scan, import or share into Linezy, sent to Google Gemini Why: Reading the receipt (section 4) Legal basis: Explicit consent
  • Google's document scanner (optional, Android only): technical data about your phone and the scanner's use, sent to Google by the scanner itself Why: Finding the edges of a receipt when you photograph it (section 7) Legal basis: Consent (article 6(1)(a); for reading this information from your phone, Estonian Electronic Communications Act § 102¹, which implements the ePrivacy Directive)
  • Names you correct, remembered for your household Why: So the next receipt from that shop shows your name for the item Legal basis: Contract
  • Warranties: the item, dates, your note, and a photo of the warranty paper if you add one Why: To keep them with the receipt. Reminders are set on your phone only Legal basis: Contract
  • Your household: its name, the name you show to it, members' roles and join dates, invitation codes (stored only in a form that can't be read back), and which receipts are private Why: Sharing receipts in the household (section 5) Legal basis: Contract
  • Splitting, if a member switches it on: the split, who paid, items marked as one person's Why: To work out who owes whom (section 5) Legal basis: Contract
  • Your scans: how many you used this month and today, whether each read worked, and what it cost us Why: Your free scans, the daily scan limit, and keeping costs and misuse under control Legal basis: Contract for the limits; our legitimate interest in controlling costs and preventing misuse
  • Your plan: free or Premium, its end date and the store it was bought in Why: Premium features Legal basis: Contract
  • Your consents: your consent to storing and your answers about AI reading and, on Android, Google's scanner, each with the time and the version of the wording you saw Why: To respect your choices and be able to show them Legal basis: Our legitimate interest in being able to show that you consented, as the law expects of us (articles 6(1)(f) and 7(1))
  • Your age confirmation: that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when Why: To show that Linezy is used only by adults Legal basis: Our legitimate interest in being able to show that we respect the minimum age
  • Withdrawals from a subscription: your name, the account's e-mail address, the store, the order number and purchase date if you give them, the time, a reference, the plan, store and end date we had on record, and what we did about it Why: To confirm your withdrawal and refund you (Terms, section 12) Legal basis: Legal obligation (Law of Obligations Act § 56⁴); our legitimate interest in being able to prove the withdrawal and the refund
  • E-mails you send us, and our replies Why: To answer you and handle your requests Legal basis: Contract, or the legal obligation to answer requests about your rights
  • Technical records: error codes, step names and counters that limit repeated attempts (for example, wrong invitation codes). No receipt content Why: Security and preventing abuse Legal basis: Our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure
  • Other people's details on your receipts, such as a cashier's name, part of a card number or a loyalty number Why: We don't read these into separate fields; they stay visible on the photo Legal basis: Our legitimate interest, and yours, in keeping your receipts as they are

What you must give us. To use Linezy you need an e-mail address to sign in, and you must agree to storing (section 4); without these we can't provide the service. Everything else is up to you: which receipts you add, whether AI reads them, whether you share them with a household.

What we don't do. We don't use your data for advertising, don't sell it, don't build marketing profiles and don't add analytics or tracking tools to the app. We don't make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects (article 22). Sorting items into categories and spotting duplicates are automatic, but you can change the result and nothing is decided about you by it.

Other people on your receipts. Receipts can show the names or numbers of other people, such as a cashier. We keep them only as part of your receipt, use them for nothing else, and can't tell those people about it, because we don't know who they are (article 14(5)(b)). Please only add receipts that are yours or that you are entitled to keep. If a receipt shows someone else's medicines, add it only if it is your own purchase or they agree.

4. Sensitive data and your two consents

Receipts can show sensitive things, such as medicines, whether they are scanned or typed in. Data that reveals health gets extra protection under the law (GDPR article 9). On the welcome screen we therefore ask for two separate explicit consents, on one page you can read through, and this policy, before you answer. Storing is what Linezy needs to work, so both buttons at the bottom of the page include it, and say so right above them. AI reading is a switch of its own that starts off: it is on only if you switch it on or tap "Allow all", and "Continue with my choices", right next to it and just the same, leaves it off. Nothing is switched on in advance.

1. Storing (needed to use Linezy). That Linezy stores your receipts for you and shows them to your household, unless you mark one private. Storing receipts is the service itself, so Linezy can't work without this consent. If you don't agree, you can delete your account right there. You decide what this covers: you choose which receipts to add, you can mark any receipt private, and you can delete any receipt at any time. To withdraw this consent completely, delete your account (section 8): we then delete your receipts. Withdrawing doesn't make what we did before unlawful. Your shared receipts are shown only to the members of your household: nobody sees them while you are alone in it. Before you invite the first person, Linezy asks whether to share the receipts you added so far or keep them private (section 5). Once others are in it, everyone in it sees the shared receipts, including people who join later, and they also see the shared receipts from before they joined. Only the household's owner can invite people.

2. Reading with AI (optional). That the photos and PDFs you add are sent to Google's Gemini to be read. If you leave it off, Linezy still works, but you can only add receipts by typing them in (typed receipts count in your spending but don't build price history): scanning, photo import and sharing into Linezy don't work. You can switch AI reading on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy and data. Switching it off means nothing new is sent to Google; the receipts you already have stay, because you agreed to storing them, and you can still delete them.

What happens when AI reading is on:

  • Only the photos or PDF of the receipt you scan, import or share into Linezy are sent, with our reading instructions. Your name, e-mail address and household are not sent.
  • Google acts as our processor, on the paid Gemini API, under Google's data processing terms. Google does not use your receipts to train or improve its products.
  • Google keeps what it receives for up to 55 days to detect and prevent abuse of its service, and then deletes it. Authorized Google staff may look at content that its systems flag as possible abuse.
  • Google may process it outside the EU, including in the USA (section 7 says how this is protected).
  • AI can read things wrongly. Linezy shows what it is unsure about, and you can correct everything.

When we change the wording of a consent, we ask you again, and we rely only on the version you agreed to.

5. Your household

Everyone starts with a household of their own. If you invite people or join someone's household, its members see its shared receipts, every line on them (including medicines), the totals and price history built from them, the warranties on them, and the names and roles of the members. They never see your e-mail address.

  • New receipts are shared with the household. You can mark any receipt private on its page at any time (only you, or only you until a date you choose, after which the household sees it again), also while you are still alone in your household. While you are alone, nobody else sees your receipts.
  • Before your first invite, while you are alone, Linezy asks whether whoever joins may see the receipts you added so far, and says how many there are. You choose: share them with the household, or keep them all private to you. Keeping them private takes effect before the invite code exists, so nobody who joins with it sees them. If you invite again while still alone, we ask again only about receipts you added since your last answer. You can still change single receipts later. Only the person who added a receipt can change who sees it.
  • If you join a household and were alone in your own, you choose whether the receipts you already have come along shared or private. If others were in your household, only your private receipts come along.
  • If you leave or are removed, you continue in a household of your own with your private receipts. Receipts you shared stay with the household and the others can still see them, because they are part of the household's records too; our basis is the legitimate interest of the other members in keeping those records, and, for anything that reveals health, the consent to storing you gave. Before you leave, you can make any receipt you added private, so that it goes with you, or delete it. The app tells you what stays before you leave or remove someone. After you have left, you can still ask us at privacy@linezy.app to delete any receipt you added, or to make it private to you; we do it without undue delay, and within one month at the latest. This is also how you withdraw your storing consent for a receipt you left with a household. Receipts left with a household are kept only while it has members: when its last member leaves it (for example by joining another household) or deletes their account, they are deleted. Your name stays in the household's splitting history for the months you were part of it, until you delete your account (our basis: the other members' legitimate interest in correct records).
  • If you delete your account, every receipt you added is deleted, including those you left with a household.
  • Splitting is off until a member switches it on. Then Linezy uses the household's shared receipts, the split the household sets, who paid (or that it was paid from joint money) and items marked as one person's to work out who owes whom. Any member can change these, and everyone sees who made a change.

6. Price sharing (not switched on)

Price sharing is not switched on, so nothing from your receipts is shared with other users. A price comparison between users is planned. Before it starts we will update this policy and ask you, and it stays off unless you say yes.

7. Who processes data for us, and transfers outside the EU

We use the service providers below. Those marked processor work only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement with us (GDPR article 28). Those marked independent controller decide themselves how they use the data, under their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) — processor What for: Database, file storage, sign-in and sending sign-in e-mails. Its logs include IP addresses and, for sign-ins, e-mail addresses Where: Stored in the EU: Frankfurt, Germany, on Amazon Web Services. Supabase's staff and some of its own providers may access data from outside the EU, for example from the USA or Singapore; this is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in Supabase's data processing agreement
  • Google (Gemini API) — processor What for: Reading a receipt photo or PDF, only with your consent (section 4) Where: May be processed in any country where Google has facilities, including the USA. Covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified, and by the standard contractual clauses in Google's data processing terms
  • Google (Google Workspace) — processor What for: Our e-mail: messages you send to support@ or privacy@linezy.app, our replies, and the e-mail receipt we send when you withdraw from a subscription Where: May be processed outside the EU, including in the USA. Covered by the Data Privacy Framework and the standard contractual clauses in Google's data processing terms
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) — processor. Used only once Premium is on sale in the app What for: Checking Premium purchases. Receives a random account number (not your name or e-mail address), your IP address, your phone's system, app version, language and country, and the store's record of the purchase Where: USA. Covered by the standard contractual clauses in RevenueCat's data processing agreement
  • Google Play and Apple App Store — independent controllers What for: Selling Premium and taking the payment Where: Under Google's and Apple's own privacy policies
  • Google ML Kit document scanner (Android only, and only if you allow it). Linezy and Google are joint controllers for collecting this technical data and sending it to Google; from then on Google is an independent controller and alone decides how it uses it. Under our arrangement, we inform you and handle your requests about it: write to privacy@linezy.app What for: Finding the edges of a receipt when you photograph it, with your consent (see below). It runs on your phone and doesn't send the photo to Google Where: Google receives technical data about your phone and the scanner's use and uses it for its own purposes, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google may process it outside the EU, including in the USA, where Google LLC is certified under the Data Privacy Framework
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) — independent controller; hosts our website and processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect its service, under its own privacy statement What for: Showing linezy.app (section 12). Not used for the app or your receipts Where: USA. Covered by the Data Privacy Framework

Google's document scanner (Android, optional). On Android, Linezy can use Google's ML Kit document scanner, which finds the edges of a receipt and straightens it. It works on your phone and never sends the photo or what is on your receipts to Google. But the scanner itself sends Google technical data: your phone model and Android version, Linezy and its version, an identifier for each installation, and performance and error data. Google uses it for its own purposes, such as improving the scanner and preventing misuse. Because this reads and sends information from your phone that a scan doesn't need, we use Google's scanner only with your consent: a switch of its own on the welcome screen, off unless you switch it on or tap "Allow all", and separate from AI reading, or the switch "Use Google's scanner" in Settings → Privacy and data. If you don't allow it, or haven't answered, your phone's camera app takes the photo and you crop it yourself on Linezy's crop screen; Google's scanner isn't started and nothing is sent to Google. You can withdraw at any time with the same switch; from then on Google's scanner isn't used and nothing new is collected, and what Google already received is under Google's own terms. On iPhone, Linezy uses Apple's scanner built into the phone, which sends neither us nor Google anything, so we don't ask there.

The Data Privacy Framework is an adequacy decision of the European Commission (GDPR article 45). The standard contractual clauses are safeguards under article 46. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at privacy@linezy.app.

We may also have to give data to a court or authority where the law requires it, and we check every such request first. If Linezy Digital OÜ ever sells or transfers the Linezy service, the new owner may take over as controller only under this policy; we will tell you before that happens, and you can delete your account first.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account and receipts: until you delete them. Keeping your receipts over time is the service you asked for (your price history), so we don't delete them while your account exists.
  • Deleting a receipt removes its photos, items and warranties, including warranty papers.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Privacy and data → Delete account and data) removes your login, your receipts with their photos, items and warranties, your scan records and consents, your household if you are its only member (with its remembered names), and every receipt you added to a household you share. Names remembered for a household that continues belong to that household and stay with it. A warranty you added to another member's receipt stays with that receipt, without your name. Your phone's copy is wiped too. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a store subscription: cancel that in the store.
  • Copies in backups: deleted data can remain in our database provider's backups for up to 7 days, until those backups are replaced. We don't restore deleted data from them except to recover from a fault, and then we delete it again.
  • Sign-in records: the IP address and phone type of each signed-in phone, until you sign out on that phone or delete your account, and at most until the phone has gone 90 days without using Linezy: it then has to sign in again (the receipts on it stay). Uninstalling the app doesn't sign you out, so sign out first. Service logs (including IP addresses, and your e-mail address when you sign in): our database provider Supabase keeps them to run and protect its service, for periods it sets, and we can't delete them ourselves; we use them only to fix faults and to look into attacks or misuse. Counters that limit repeated attempts (such as wrong invitation codes): kept for one hour, then removed within 10 minutes.
  • Google Gemini: up to 55 days, as described in section 4.
  • Withdrawals from a subscription: 3 years after the withdrawal, so we can prove it and the refund if there is a dispute (the general limitation period for contract claims). If we paid the refund ourselves, only the details the accounting record needs (date, amount, order number and reference) are kept for 7 years, as the Estonian Accounting Act requires; the rest is deleted after 3 years. They are kept after you delete your account, with your name, e-mail address and purchase details, but no link to your Linezy data.
  • E-mails you send us: about a complaint, a refund or your rights, 3 years after our last reply; other e-mails, 1 year after our last reply.
  • RevenueCat's purchase records are deleted when you delete your account. Google Play and Apple keep their own records of your purchases.
  • Our shop and product catalog: when receipts are read, we keep shop names and addresses, and product names as printed, in a catalog that helps match prices. After your receipts are deleted, these entries are no longer linked to you or your household and can't be seen by other users.

On your phone, Linezy keeps a copy of your receipts so it works without a connection. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account. On Android it is left out of phone backups. On iPhone it can be included in your phone's own backup (such as iCloud Backup), which is under your Apple account.

Deleting your account without the app: e-mail privacy@linezy.app from the address you sign in with, and we delete the account and its data within one month and confirm it to you.

9. Your rights

In the app you can:

  • see all your receipts and download their details (Export: Excel or CSV, or a PDF summary, for the newest 2,000 receipts; for more, or for your photos, write to us);
  • correct anything that was read wrongly;
  • delete any receipt, or your whole account and its data;
  • switch AI reading on or off, and on Android, Google's scanner.

You also have the right to ask us for access to your data and a copy of it, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and portability (a copy in a machine-readable format, including your photos). You can withdraw a consent at any time; this doesn't affect what we did before. Write to privacy@linezy.app. It is free. We answer within one month; if a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months and tell you why within the first month. To protect your data, we may ask you to write from the e-mail address of your account or otherwise confirm it's you.

Your right to object. Wherever we rely on legitimate interest (including sign-in records, controlling costs and misuse, technical records, your consent and age records, shared receipts that stay after you leave a household, your name in splitting history, withdrawal records, the website and other people's details on your receipts), you can object at any time at privacy@linezy.app, on grounds relating to your situation. We then stop, unless we show compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, info@aki.ee, www.aki.ee), or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to fix things first, but you don't have to ask us before you complain.

10. Security

Data is encrypted on its way between the app and our servers, and stored by Supabase on encrypted disks. Each household can reach only its own receipts: this is enforced in the database, not only in the app. Photos are in private storage. Our keys to Google and to the payment services are kept on the server, never in the app. Only the people at Linezy who need it have access to our systems, and we look at the content of your receipts only when you ask us for help with them or the law requires it.

If a personal data breach happens, we tell the Data Protection Inspectorate within 72 hours where the law requires it, and tell you without undue delay if it is likely to put you at high risk.

11. Age

Linezy is only for people aged 18 or older, because the AI service we use requires it. This applies to every account, whether or not you use AI reading. You confirm your age on the welcome screen. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it and its data, and refund any Premium paid for it. Please tell us at privacy@linezy.app if you know of one.

12. This website

The website linezy.app has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking, and it loads nothing from other servers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the page you ask for, and logs them to deliver the page and protect its service against attacks; it keeps those logs under its own privacy statement. We don't receive these records. Our basis is our legitimate interest in having a secure website.

13. Changes to this policy

Before a change to this policy takes effect, we e-mail you about it, and for an important change, such as a new purpose, a new kind of data or a new recipient, we do so at least 30 days ahead. If a change needs your consent, we ask for it, and nothing changes for you until you agree. The version date is at the top. You can ask us for an earlier version at privacy@linezy.app.

14. Language

This policy is in English and Estonian. Both versions have the same meaning and are equally valid. If they ever differ, the version that is more favorable to you applies.
